# Results log

One entry per experiment, newest last. Every number points at its source file under `runs/`; the pre-registered
criteria and their outcomes are in `docs/preregistration.md`. Detection is always "share of marked texts flagged at a
1% false-positive threshold frozen on clean text"; preference is the judge's win rate of the marked text against the
clean text of the same prompt, both orders, ties = 0.5, and is always read against the measured clean-vs-clean level.

## Corrections on the record
- "The segment statistic is bit-identical under sentence reordering" (pre-registered for entry 13) was wrong as
  stated: it is invariant to permuting its own W-word units, but reordering sentences re-cuts those units. Measured
  retention 70-78% vs 15-18% for the chained statistic.
- The beta for entry 13 was chosen from the projection sweep without reading any steered text; the plan's "text still
  reads normally" condition was skipped. Do not fix a strength without reading samples.
- Dev prompts (60, reused many times) overstated quality twice (entries 1 and 9); quality claims come from held-out
  prompts only.
